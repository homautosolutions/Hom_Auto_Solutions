Our Story

Founded in 2022, H.O.M Auto Solutions was created with a clear and singular purpose: to raise safety standards for Indian motorcycle riders.

Recognizing the gap between global motorsport technologies and local availability, we built an ecosystem connecting international riding gear brands with passionate riders across the subcontinent.
